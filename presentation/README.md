# Live pitch materials

Related: [[PITCH]] · [[DATA]] · [Backend verification](../docs/VERIFICATION.md)

- `Verity-Live-Pitch-v2.pptx`: editable PowerPoint, eight main slides and three appendix slides,
  with speaker notes. Planned five-minute delivery includes a live demo.
- `PITCH.md`: exact five-minute and three-minute scripts, demo cues, fallback wording and judge Q&A.
- `DATA.md` and `synthetic-examples.jsonl`: eight original fictional illustrations. These are
  expected labels, not model predictions, training records or benchmark results.
- `evaluation-template.csv`: blank baseline-versus-adapter evaluation worksheet.

These files preserve the earlier research-plan pitch. Since that draft, the model-independent
backend has been implemented and tested. Frontend and real model integration are still pending.
The checkpoint remains a candidate, fine-tuning remains proposed, and no measured improvement is
claimed. Update the slides and script from actual model/training results before the final pitch.
The backend's scripted demo results must be labeled as fixtures during rehearsal or presentation.

No real student records are included. The dataset is illustration-only and excluded from training
and scored evaluation. The PPTX itself can be edited directly in PowerPoint or Google Slides.
